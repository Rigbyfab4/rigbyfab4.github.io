About

The floor-to-data story
behind the work

Most analytics leaders come up through data. I came up through people — through customers, merchandise tables, store floors, and the day-to-day reality of specialty retail. That grounding is what makes the difference.

I started my career at L.L.Bean in a customer-facing store role, progressed through the full arc of retail merchandising, then made a deliberate pivot into data and analytics — carrying everything I'd learned about how the business actually works into every dashboard, dataset, and recommendation since.

Today I lead the Customer Data Stewardship team, overseeing how L.L.Bean's customer data is governed, protected, and strategically activated. I was a founding Day 0 member of Marketing Modernization — a cross-functional program reimagining how technology, strategy, and human insight come together to center the customer in every decision.

What sets me apart is not just technical skill. It's the ability to bridge data systems and business reality, lead teams with empathy and clarity, and turn complex information into decisions that actually move the needle.
